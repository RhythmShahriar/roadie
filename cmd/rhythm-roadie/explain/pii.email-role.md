# pii.email-role: a role address such as noreply@ (info)

What: An address with a role local part (noreply, no-reply, donotreply,
mailer-daemon, postmaster, wordpress). It names a mailbox, not a person.

Why it matters: Role addresses are rarely personal, so they are listed for
information and never fail a run on their own.

What Roadie does: Nothing: they are kept.

By hand: Nothing to do, unless the address belongs to a person.
