# pii.address: a real-looking street address (medium)

What: A value in a street column (address_1, address_2, street, address) that
isn't from Roadie's lists.

Why it matters: A street address locates a person.

What Roadie does: Street, city and postcode columns get fakes; country and
state are kept by default for tax and shipping rules.

By hand: Add the column under columns: with rule: street or address.
